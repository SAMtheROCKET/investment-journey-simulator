# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Investment Journey Simulator is intended to be a welcoming, constructive and
technically rigorous open-source project.

When participating in this project, please:

- treat other contributors and users with respect,
- discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people,
- welcome questions from beginners as well as advanced users,
- give constructive technical feedback,
- acknowledge uncertainty and limitations,
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation or deliberately disruptive
  behaviour,
- and respect the privacy of others.

Different views on modelling assumptions, financial conventions, software
architecture and numerical methods are welcome. Disagreement should remain
focused on the work.

## Financial and numerical discussions

Because this project deals with financial simulation, claims about calculations,
tax treatment, historical data or numerical behaviour should be supported where
practical by reproducible examples, independent calculations or authoritative
sources.

Good-faith mistakes are part of technical work. The goal is to identify,
understand and correct them transparently.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to participation in the project's repositories,
issues, pull requests, discussions and other official project spaces.

## Enforcement

Behaviour that violates these standards may be edited, removed or rejected.
Participants who repeatedly or seriously violate them may be temporarily or
permanently restricted from project spaces.

## Reporting

For ordinary moderation concerns, contact the project maintainer privately
rather than escalating personal disputes in public threads.

Security vulnerabilities should instead be reported through the repository's
private security-reporting process.

Reports will be reviewed as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible.

# India resident - the setup checklist

For someone resident in India opening their first investment
account. The order matters: several steps block the ones after them.

---

## 1. The documents that gate everything

| Document | Why it blocks things |
|---|---|
| **PAN** | Nothing financial happens without it. Get this first. |
| **Aadhaar** | Drives the fastest KYC route, and links to your bank. |
| **Bank account** | Must be in your own name; investments settle to it. |
| **Address proof** | Must match what you give KYC, exactly. |

**The single most common delay** is a mismatch - a name spelled
differently across PAN, Aadhaar and bank, or an address updated in
one place and not another. Reconcile all three *before* starting
KYC, not after it bounces.

---

## 2. KYC

KYC is done once and then reused across every registered
intermediary, which is why it is worth getting right the first time.

- Complete it through a KRA (KYC Registration Agency) or directly
  with the platform you intend to use.
- The Aadhaar-based route is usually fastest.
- **Check your status afterwards.** A KYC that is "registered" but
  not "validated" will still block some transactions, and the
  difference is not always explained clearly on screen.

---

## 3. Choosing how to hold funds

**Direct or Regular.** Regular plans embed a distributor
commission in the expense ratio; Direct plans do not. Over a long
horizon that difference is substantial - put both expense ratios
into the Advanced Simulator and look at the gap before deciding
that the advice is worth it. Sometimes it is. It should be a
decision, not a default.

**Demat or not.** Mutual fund units can be held in statement form
without a demat account. You need demat for stocks and ETFs.

---

## 4. Setting up the actual investment

- Register on your chosen platform, or directly with the AMC.
- Complete the bank mandate for automatic debits. **This has a
  lead time** - usually days, sometimes weeks - so set it up before
  the date you want your first instalment to run.
- Set the SIP date a few days after payday, not before it.
- Nominate a beneficiary. It takes two minutes and saves your
  family months.

---

## 5. Tax, in one paragraph

Equity-oriented funds held over the long-term threshold are taxed at
the long-term rate with an annual exemption **per taxpayer, per
year** - not per fund, which is the mistake most calculators make.
Below that threshold, the short-term rate applies. Debt funds
follow their own treatment. A surcharge may apply above certain
income levels, with marginal relief near the thresholds.

**This portal models all of that in full**, including grandfathering
and loss carry-forward. Enter your income on the Advanced Simulator
and the surcharge is applied properly rather than ignored.

---

## 6. Once a year

- Check your KYC is still valid and your address current.
- Review the actual expense ratios you are paying.
- Rebalance if you meant to - and see the Rebalancing Lab first,
  because the tax on the trade is real and often larger than the
  benefit.
- Increase the SIP by whatever your income rose by.
